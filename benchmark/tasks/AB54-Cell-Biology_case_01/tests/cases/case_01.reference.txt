The three Example7 assays provide distinct cargo-dependent observations: pHrodo-labeledA-beta1-42, labeledPGPC/POPC lipids, and apoptoticSHSY5Y cells. Each uses murineBMDMs,20ug/mL antibody, and measurements over1–3h. pHrodo fluorescence depends on delivery to acidic compartments and their acidification. An increase is therefore not a direct measurement of the mass of internalized intact cargo, and effects on lysosomal pH or cargo degradation can contribute.

A common TREM2-dependent mechanism is not established by an antibody-associated change alone. Species engagement is a specific limitation: Table2 reports no mouse binding for02M10, while22A17 and23E21-2 were not tested for mouse binding. Fc-receptor signaling and other pathways remain possible explanations. Validate mouse-target engagement, then compare the response in TREM2-deficient or blocked BMDMs using suitable matched antibody controls to test target dependence.

Use pH-insensitive cargo imaging or an orthogonal internalization assay alongside an acidification control to separate uptake from compartment-pH changes; Fc-silent or matched isotype controls can assess Fc contributions. Finally, repeat the defined cargo endpoint in human microglia. Example6 measures solubleTREM2,CD11b and confluence in hiPSC-derived microglia after72h, not these three phagocytosis endpoints, so it does not establish cross-species functional equivalence.
