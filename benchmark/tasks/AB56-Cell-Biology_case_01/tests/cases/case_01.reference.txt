The observed EC50 values do not support a monotonic rule that PD-L1-high cells must give greater degranulation potency. For ADT5-1, A431 values are20 and18.4pM, while PD-L1-low A549 values are11.7 and7.1pM in the two donors. This comparison is between cell lines, so density is confounded with other target-cell properties and is not an isolated density experiment. Activity on PD-L1-low A549 does not establish target independence because low expression is still expression. Loss of measurable activity on the A549 PD-L1-knockout line supports PD-L1 dependence under the tested conditions, especially with the anti-RSV control, while avoiding a universal mechanistic claim. Values reported as>1000pM remain right-censored and cannot be assigned exactly1000pM or used as ordinary finite points. The source incubation-time wording is inconsistent, so the protocol duration should be clarified before reproducing or pooling experiments. The corrected conclusion is that the panel shows PD-L1-associated, donor-dependent degranulation whose potency is not ordered solely by nominal high/low expression labels. Use an isogenic inducible PD-L1 density series to test density dependence, and rescue PD-L1 expression in knockout A549 with matched quantitative surface measurements to test specificity and isolate cell-line effects.
