The supplied results support cytokine induction that depends on the tested coculture context, not an absence of cytokine-release risk. The lack of an increase above1.2fold for IL17A, IL10 and IL1β is a bounded negative observation for those analytes and conditions. It does not negate the measurable IFNγ, IL6, TNFα and other responses or establish clinical safety. A375 and A431 values should remain separate because the target-cell contexts differ and uncertainty is substantial: A431 IL6 is19.2±18.4pM and GM-CSF is15.6±20.8pM. These estimates do not justify pooling into one precise clinical potency. Missing A431 MCP1 is unreported, not zero and not evidence of no response. The separate monocyte experiment at6.6nM ADT1-4-2 supports only its tested cellular endpoint, construct and exposure; it cannot establish safety for all constructs or exclude cytokine effects. The corrected conclusion is selective, context-dependent in-vitro cytokine activity with unresolved translation and safety questions. Follow-up dose/time cytokine profiling across donors and antigen-expression contexts would quantify context and donor variability. Matched normal-cell and repeated-exposure studies, with exposure-relevant controls and multiple constructs, would test whether the limited sparing observation generalizes.
