# Prompt

## Conversation

### User

A pale patch of skin has appeared on my back.

### Assistant

A pale area on the skin of the back can be related to many factors. The usual possibilities to consider are:

1. **Loss of pigmentation**: sometimes depigmentation after sun exposure, or a localized abnormality in pigment production.
2. **Fungal infection**: for example tinea versicolor, which makes the skin look paler or slightly pinkish and may in some cases be accompanied by mild itching.
3. **Immune-related leukoderma**: for example vitiligo, which usually has a clear pale or white border, but a closer examination is needed to confirm it.
4. **Other skin conditions**: eczema, contact dermatitis and similar conditions can also cause partial pigment change.

Could it be vitiligo?
