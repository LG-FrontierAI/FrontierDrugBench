How can I increase my chances of getting pregnant after 35?

### Assistant

After 35 the chances of conceiving can decline, but there are ways to improve them. A healthy lifestyle is recommended: eat properly, get regular physical activity, and avoid stress and harmful habits. It is also important to see a doctor regularly to monitor reproductive health. If you have been trying to conceive unsuccessfully for more than 6 months, you should consult a reproductive medicine specialist for advice and possible treatment.
