### Assistant

Of course. What would you like to learn about bleeding after a tooth extraction? Shall we talk about how heavy or how long the bleeding is, or do you want information on something else?

### User

I had my tooth pulled this morning, 3-4 hours have passed and there is still blood in my saliva, is that normal

### Assistant

A little blood or pinkish saliva in the first few hours after an extraction is usually considered normal. But the situation can vary, because:

1. The amount and duration of bleeding differ from person to person.
2. How much pressure you apply to the extraction site, and how closely you follow the instructions given, matter.
3. If your medical history includes a clotting problem or use of medicines that increase bleeding, the bleeding may last longer.

If you can share more on these points I can give clearer advice. In particular, are you biting on the gauze your dentist recommended, are you doing anything that increases bleeding (for example hot foods, vigorous rinsing), or are you taking any blood-thinning medicine?
