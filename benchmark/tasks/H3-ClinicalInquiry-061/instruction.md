# Prompt

## Conversation

### User

I'm getting a flu shot and I'm wondering about the timing.

### Assistant

Here is a short summary of flu vaccination:

1. **Generally recommended timing**
   - Vaccination is usually recommended between late September and October.
   - It is best to leave about two weeks for antibodies to build up before the flu season arrives.

2. **How long protection lasts**
   - Protection usually lasts about six months after vaccination.
   - Flu activity is highest in winter, so getting vaccinated in autumn is preferable.

3. **Whether you were vaccinated before**
   - A newly updated vaccine is recommended every year.
   - Even if you had one last year, you need it again this year.

The exact timing can be adjusted to your circumstances and health.
