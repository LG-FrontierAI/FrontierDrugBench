### User

Why do people take muscle supplements?

### Assistant

The main reason people take muscle supplements is to improve exercise performance and muscle mass. Some supplements support protein synthesis or the use of energy sources, and can contribute to strength and physical performance.

The main muscle supplements are:

1. **Protein supplements (whey, casein, etc.)**
   - Provide the amino acids needed for muscle repair and growth.

2. **Amino acid supplements (BCAA, EAA, etc.)**
   - May help prevent muscle loss and promote recovery.

3. **Creatine**
   - Helps regenerate ATP, the energy source inside muscle, so you can produce more force during exercise, and may improve performance in short bouts of high-intensity work.

4. **Beta-alanine**
   - Can reduce lactate accumulation in muscle and raise exercise endurance.

5. **Other supplements (HMB, glutamine, etc.)**
   - May help in specific situations such as limiting muscle loss or supporting immunity.

However, the form and the amount that suit a person differ from one individual to another. Safe use means taking your current health, your training goal and your diet into account together.
