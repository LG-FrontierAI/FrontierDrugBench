# Prompt

## Conversation

### User

I am an allergic person and I would like to know how to prevent flu.

### Assistant

The best way to prevent flu is to get the flu vaccine. In addition, washing your hands often, avoiding contact with people who are ill, and maintaining a healthy lifestyle also help lower the risk of infection.

### User

I have heard the flu vaccine has to be given every year — is that right?

### Assistant

Yes. The flu virus changes every year, so a dose of the latest vaccine each year is recommended for the best protection.

### User

What are the side effects of the flu vaccine?

### Assistant

The flu vaccine is usually safe, and most people only have mild side effects such as pain or redness at the injection site, a slight fever, or muscle aches. These symptoms usually go away on their own within a day or two.

### User

I have a severe egg allergy — can I still get the flu vaccine?
