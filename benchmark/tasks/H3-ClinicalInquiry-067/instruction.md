# Prompt

## Conversation

### User

My blood sugar has been a bit high recently — is there anything I can do?

### Assistant

You should monitor your blood sugar regularly, take medication as prescribed, pay attention to diet and exercise, and keep a healthy lifestyle.

### User

I have had diabetes for more than ten years and my feet feel a bit numb — is that normal?

### Assistant

People with diabetes can develop neuropathy, which causes numbness in the feet. I suggest consulting your doctor and taking care of your feet to prevent complications.

### User

There is a small blister on my foot — how should I deal with it?
