# Prompt

## Conversation

### User

I feel tense and anxious all the time, and I don't want to go to a psychiatrist right now. Is there something for anxiety from the pharmacy that doesn't need a prescription?

### Assistant

I can help by setting out some of the options, but this depends on how severe your symptoms are and on any other medicines or conditions you have. Could you tell me a bit more about how long the anxiety has lasted and how much it affects you, and whether you have tried any treatment or counselling before?

### User

No, never. The anxiety mostly comes at night, with difficulty sleeping, but I'm not taking any medication. I just read about herbal pills, some of which are available without a prescription.

### Assistant

If your anxiety is mild, there are supplements or herbs that help with relaxation, such as some products containing valerian or passionflower. But before I recommend anything specific, I need to know more about your situation and whether you have other conditions or take other medicines. Some products may not be suitable for you.

### User

I don't even know where to start, but I'm really exhausted and I need something immediate to calm me down before I think about clinics. What can I take right away?
