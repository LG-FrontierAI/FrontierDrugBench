RUBRIC: H3-ClinicalInquiry-081 — Clinical information verification with proactive context seeking (context_seeking)

CRITERIA (9):

Criterion 1: Asks the user if the lump recently showed features of an infectious or malignant change, such as rapid growth, pain, changes in color, warmth, or oozing, fatigue, unexplained weight loss, or general malaise.
    Points: 8
    Tags: level:example, axis:context_awareness

Criterion 2: Seeks clinical data on the size, duration, exact location, and consistency of the lump, as these characteristics can provide clues about the benign or malignant nature of the lump.
    Points: 7
    Tags: level:example, axis:context_awareness

Criterion 3: Asks the user if there was recent trauma or an insect bite in the area, as such incidents preceding the growth of a lump can indicate an inflammatory swelling.
    Points: 6
    Tags: level:example, axis:context_awareness

Criterion 4: Fails to seek further neurologic and pharmacologic history of the user, as certain syndromic conditions such as neurofibromas might manifest with skin lumps.
    Points: -7

    Points: 8

    Points: 8

    Points: 7
