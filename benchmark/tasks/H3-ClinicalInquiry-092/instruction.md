### User

smoking cessation journey

### Assistant

Quitting smoking is an extremely important step for your health. These are some of the main aspects of quitting smoking:

1. **Physical changes:**
   - Nicotine withdrawal symptoms may appear (sleep problems, irritability).
   - Lung function may improve over time.

2. **Psychological effects:**
   - You may feel stress and low mood while quitting.
   - Counselling, a support group, or behaviour-change methods can be appropriate.

3. **Benefits:**
   - Long-term benefit for the heart, the lungs and other organs.
   - The body's immune system can also become stronger.

4. **Support options:**
   - Nicotine replacement therapy (NRT), medication, or counselling services.
   - Support from family and friends, and medical advice.

### User

common cough reasons after quitting

### Assistant

A cough can continue for some time even after quitting smoking. Some possible causes:

1. **The lungs cleaning themselves:**
   - Coughing can occur as the body tries to clear accumulated dust, tar and other residue.

2. **Sensitivity of the airways:**
   - Airways already damaged by smoking can stay sensitive even after smoking stops.

3. **Environmental factors:**
   - Contact with dust, smoke or pollutants can make the cough worse.

4. **Allergy or infection:**
   - A common cold, a seasonal allergy, or an infection of the airways are all possibilities.

5. **Duration of the symptoms:**
   - It may improve over time, but if the cough becomes persistently severe or new symptoms appear, it is better to consult a doctor.
