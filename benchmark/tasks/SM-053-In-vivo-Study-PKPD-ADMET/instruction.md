# Prompt

Total: 100 points.

A drug discovery program aims to develop an orally administered small-molecule kinase inhibitor for the treatment of a chronic disease. The target product profile (TPP) requires once-daily (QD) oral dosing and long-term administration. Following an initial round of analog synthesis, the project team has identified lead compound A and must decide whether to invest further resources in advancing it toward preclinical candidate compound (PCC) selection.

The physicochemical properties and selected in vitro ADMET data for compound A are provided below:

SMILES: CN(CC1)CCN1C(C=C2)=CC=C2C3=CN=C(NC=C4C5=NNC=C5)C4=C3
Molecular formula: C21H22N6
Exact mass: 358.19
cLogP: 2.737 (calculated using ChemDraw)
tPSA: 55.26 Å² (calculated using ChemDraw)
pKa: 8.57 (calculated using ChemDraw)
Fsp3: 0.24 (calculated)
Cellular IC50: 100 nM
Thermodynamic solubility: 20.6 µM at pH 7.4; 78.4 µM at pH 2.0
MDCK-MDR1 Papp A-B [efflux ratio]: 2.4 × 10⁻⁶ cm/s [27]
Caco-2 Papp A-B [efflux ratio]: 15 × 10⁻⁶ cm/s [2.2]
HLM/RLM/MLM T1/2 (min): 23/2.5/4.3
CYP (1A2/2B6/2C8/2C9/2C19/2D6/3A-T/3A-M) inhibition: > 10 µM
hERG inhibition: > 10 µM

Mouse PK data are provided below:

IV at 3 mg/kg:
Cmax: 485 ng/mL
AUC0-t: 294 h·ng/mL
t1/2: 0.9 h
CLobs: 172 mL/min/kg
Vss,obs: 7.97 L/kg

PO at 10 mg/kg:
Tmax: 0.4 h
Cmax: 230 ng/mL
AUC0-t: 537 h·ng/mL
t1/2: 1 h
MRT: 2.23 h
F: 54.7%

Plasma protein binding (PPB), unbound fraction (fu) in human/rat/mouse: 2.5%/1.7%/3.1%

(1) Given a cellular IC50 of 100 nM, assess whether PO dosing at 10 mg/kg QD is feasible in a mouse efficacy model. Provide a quantitative rationale.
(2) Considering all the available data, should compound A be advanced as a lead for further optimization? Explain your decision and, if you recommend continued advancement, identify the properties that should be prioritized for optimization.
