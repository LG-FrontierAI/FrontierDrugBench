DC50 describes concentration potency; Dmax is the reported maximum percentage of target-protein loss. Classify both attributes jointly. Comparisons and ordering use DC50 only among compounds whose measured Dmax is at least 80%.

Use each molecule's assay context. In the tables below, P denotes its stated IC50, EC50, DC50, qAC50 or CD, expressed in nM. Lower P means greater concentration potency.
